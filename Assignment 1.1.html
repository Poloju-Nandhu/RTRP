MALLA REDDY ENGINEERING COLLEGE AND MANAGEMENT SCIENCES

Malla Reddy Engineering College And Management Sciences (MREM) is an Engineering and Management college in Hyderabad, Telangana,established in 2008. It's affiliated with Jewaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH). MREM offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs, including B.Tech, M.Tech, and MBA, with admissions through TS EAMCET, GATE, and TS ICET respectively. The college is known for its emphasis on technical education and industry-aligned training.
